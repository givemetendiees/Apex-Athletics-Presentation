The Negatives

120%

Reliance on overtime

All three active plants ran at 120% of regular-time capacity, producing 2.4 million pairs on overtime.

131,000

Missed wholesale demand

North America lost an estimated 131,000 wholesale pairs to stockouts.

$34.8M

Lower private-label revenue

Revenue fell from $68.5M to $34.8M. Compare private-label contribution per pair with what the same capacity could earn in branded footwear.

Apex expanded capacity, but supply still fell short in North America.

11 / BOARD PRESENTATION

Future Plans Years 19–20

YEAR 19

Test and improve

  • Improve product availability
    Review regional inventory allocation and compare the cost of overtime with regular-time capacity.
  • Sharpen the customer offer
    Test price and assortment changes. Track market share and profit per pair.
  • Protect shareholder returns
    Cover branded demand first. Evaluate private-label orders on contribution per pair.
YEAR 20

Expand what works

  • Improve product availability
    Expand where recurring demand and the expected return justify the investment.
  • Sharpen the customer offer
    Extend changes that improve demand and profitability.
  • Protect shareholder returns
    Review EPS, ROE and credit rating before committing more capital.

Set numeric targets once Year 18 actuals are available.
