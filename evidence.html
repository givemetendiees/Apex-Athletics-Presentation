Quality improved.
Availability held us back.

CHANNEL MIX

+$37.7M revenue

Internet revenue rose 17.3% to $141.6M. Private-label returned with $45.0M, while wholesale fell 6.2% to $421.9M.

PRODUCTION

120% utilization

Both plants used full overtime on 8.5M pairs of equipment capacity. No equipment or facility space was added; no new space is underway for Year 14.

CUSTOMER APPEAL

8.0–8.2-star S/Q

Quality rose from 6.5 stars in every region and image rating reached 90. Wholesale market share fell in all four regions.

Channel growth came with operating tradeoffs

05 / LESSONS & NEXT-ROUND PRIORITIES

The next challenge:
profitable customer appeal.

Historical Year 14 proposals based on Year 13 evidence · see the latest financial and operating results below

VARIETY

Validate the narrower range

Apex offered 64–87 models versus industry averages of 308–322. Test whether stronger quality offsets the shrinking selection for our intended customers.

AVAILABILITY

Recover unmet demand

Wholesale stockout losses rose from 263,000 pairs to 646,000. At full overtime utilization, review capacity, regional allocation, and the branded/private-label production mix.

CUSTOMER VALUE

Turn quality into returns

Protect the gains in quality and image while restoring wholesale margins in North America and Europe-Africa. Assess the return on advertising and the price/value offer by region.

Competitive context: the gap to Dynasty Kicks

Year 13 historical benchmark: Apex annual score 96; Dynasty Kicks 109.

Sources & interpretation

Year 13 wholesale stockout losses: North America 240,000 pairs; Europe-Africa 170,000; Asia-Pacific 183,000; Latin America 53,000. North America brand advertising was $18.5M versus the $11.556M industry average, while model availability was 72.7% below average. These priorities are recommendations, not recorded management decisions or a Year 19–20 forecast.

Year 16 · What the Board may ask

Where did growth come from? Internet revenue rose from $217.466M to $275.778M; wholesale from $621.246M to $725.692M. Private-label fell from $68.474M to $34.827M.

Did capacity relieve the constraint? Asia-Pacific added 3.25M pairs of regular-time equipment capacity and Latin America added 2.0M. Despite the expansion, production still used 2.4M overtime pairs. North America lost 131,000 wholesale pairs to stockouts.

How did Apex compare with Dynasty Kicks? Annual weighted score: 103 versus 107. Game-to-date score: 107 versus 113. Both earned an annual investor expectation score of 118; the annual best-in-industry score was 87 for Apex versus 95 for Dynasty.

Sources: Year 16 Performance Highlights p. 1, Facilities & Equipment and Production & Workforce, Comparative Competitive Efforts (North America), and FIR Scoreboard.
